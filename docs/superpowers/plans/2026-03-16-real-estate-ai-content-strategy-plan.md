# Real Estate AI Content Strategy: 30-Day Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (if subagents available) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Establish presence on Xiaohongshu, Instagram, and Threads targeting overseas buyer real estate agents; interview older sister to identify the #1 pain point to solve; start build-in-public; end Month 1 with a working MVP delivered to older sister and at least one inbound inquiry from a non-sister agent.

**Architecture:** Research-in-public → interview older sister → identify highest-pain problem → build AI tool for her → document every step as content. Each action serves double duty: it moves the build forward AND generates a post. This plan covers Days 1–30, which includes the full research phase and the first half of the build phase. The second half of the build phase (Week 5–6) and case study → client acquisition are Month 2.

**Platforms:** Xiaohongshu (RED) in **Simplified Chinese** (mainland Chinese buyer audience), Instagram + Threads in **Traditional Chinese** (Taiwanese buyer + agent audience). No video in Month 1. Manual posting — no automation yet.

---

## Platform Format Guide

Use this for every post decision:

| Platform | Language | Format | Length | Tone |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Xiaohongshu (RED) | Simplified Chinese | Long-form note or photo essay with headers + lists | 300–800 chars | Structured, informative, authentic |
| Instagram | Traditional Chinese | Carousel (3–5 slides) or single photo with caption | Caption: 150–300 chars | Visual-first, clear slide text |
| Threads | Traditional Chinese | Short conversational post or thread | 100–300 chars | Informal, personal, question-driven |

Do NOT copy-paste the same post to all three platforms. Adapt format and register per platform, even when the underlying idea is the same.

## Chunk 2: Research Phase — Interview Older Sister (Days 8–14)

### Task 5: Prepare and Conduct Sister Interview

**Contingency:** If sister is unavailable before Day 10, post a "still in research mode" bridge post on Day 10 (share audience replies collected so far). Reschedule interview to Day 12 at latest. If Day 12 also slips, the Day 13 announcement post must be delayed — do not announce a build before completing the interview.

- [ ] **Step 1: Schedule a 90-minute call with older sister (book by Day 7)**
  - Block uninterrupted time
  - Record the call (with her permission) for reference

- [ ] **Step 2: Finalize and save the interview question list before the call**
  Stored in `real-estate-research.md` under `## Sister Interview Questions`. Do not start the interview without reviewing this list.

  **Workflow questions:**
  1. Walk me through a typical week — what do you spend the most time on?
  2. What's the task you dread most every Monday morning?
  3. How many active leads are you managing at once? How do you track them?

  **Pain point questions:**
  4. What happens when an overseas buyer goes quiet for 2–3 weeks? Walk me through exactly what you do.
  5. How do you handle inquiries that come in at 2am your time (which is daytime in China/Taiwan)?
  6. When a buyer needs to sign a contract urgently, what's the hardest part of getting them across the line?
  7. How do you explain the contract and strata report to buyers who don't read English well?

  **Tools questions:**
  8. What CRM or tools do you use today? What do you love / hate about them?
  9. What do you currently do manually that you wish was automatic?
  10. Have you tried any AI tools? What happened?

  **Value questions:**
  11. If I could save you 5 hours a week, what would you want me to automate first?
  12. What would make you trust an AI system to handle buyer communication?
  13. How much would a tool that solved [top pain] be worth to you per month?

- [ ] **Step 3: Conduct the interview**
  - Take notes in real time
  - After the call: write down the 3 biggest pain points ranked by how much frustration came through

- [ ] **Step 4: Document findings in `real-estate-research.md`**

### Task 6: Architecture Scoping (Days 11–12)

**Do this before the Day 13 announcement.** Announcing a build before knowing what you're building technically risks publicly committing to a timeline that slips.

- [ ] **Step 5: Define the tool's job in one sentence**
  - If you can't say it in one sentence, you don't know what you're building yet. Block until this is clear.

- [ ] **Step 6: Pick the simplest architecture that solves the problem**
  Options by complexity — default to the simplest that works:
  - **A) Claude API + Google Sheet** → generates a draft message she copies and pastes (simplest, start here)
  - **B) n8n/Make workflow** → auto-generates and sends via email or WhatsApp
  - **C) Full web app** → Month 3 territory, do not build this in Month 1

- [ ] **Step 7: Define MVP "done" criteria**
  - What does sister do manually today?
  - What does she do after this tool exists?
  - What does success look like after 2 weeks of her using it?

## Daily Rhythm (Throughout Month 1)

**Every day (15–20 min):**
- Check replies/comments on all 3 platforms
- Respond to every comment in Weeks 1–2 (builds algorithm trust + real relationships)
- Save interesting replies to `real-estate-research.md`

**Posting cadence — minimum 3x/week per platform:**
- Mix buyer-facing and agent-facing posts; don't post exclusively to one audience
- RED: lean buyer-heavy (70% buyer, 30% agent) — that's who discovers new accounts there
- Instagram/Threads: lean agent-heavy (60% agent, 40% buyer) — Taiwanese audience skews toward agents + diaspora

**Weekly (Sunday, 30 min):**
- Review what performed best this week
- Identify 1 insight from the week to amplify next week
- Check: on track for the week's success criteria?

## What NOT to Do in Month 1

- **No video** — defer YouTube/Reels to Month 2 after validating demand
- **No automation** — manual posting only; don't build posting scripts until content is working
- **No perfectionism on posts** — publish before you feel ready; authenticity > polish, especially on RED
- **No scope creep on the build** — build the one thing sister said was most painful, nothing else
- **No LinkedIn** — defer to Month 2–3 when you have a case study
- **Don't announce the build before completing the architecture scoping** — public timelines that slip undermine credibility
