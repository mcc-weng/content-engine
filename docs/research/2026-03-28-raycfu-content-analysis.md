## Content Format Breakdown

### Video Style
- **Talking head** to camera (primary)
- **Screen recordings** for tutorials
- ~15-39 second duration for Reels/TikTok
- 6-17 minutes for YouTube long-form

### Audio
- TikTok: "remy's original sound" (recent) or "Rafael Krux's Classical Cello Solo (Acoustic)" (older)
- No trending audio — uses original or royalty-free background music

### Hook Formula (from captions)
1. **Contrarian/Provocative:** "Claude finally replaced Openclaw, is this the end?"
2. **Money promise:** "3 Claude plugins that you can build and sell for $5k/month"
3. **Numbered list:** "8 Claude skills I tested that actually matter"
4. **Story hook:** "He cured his dog's cancer with $3000 and ChatGPT"
5. **How-to:** "How to use Claude Dispatch in 3 steps!"
6. **Fear/FOMO:** "3 ways to protect your job from being taken over by AI"

### CTA Patterns
- "Follow if you're also in tech" (end of video)
- "Comment [keyword] for the link/prompt" (drives engagement + captures leads)
- "Save this if you're planning to..." (drives saves)
- Links to free guides on raycfu.com via keyword automation

### Caption Style
- Always starts with the hook (same as spoken hook)
- Includes `#ai #tech` on every post
- Occasionally adds partner tags (#FigmaPartner, #boltpartner)
- Long descriptive captions on story-based content
