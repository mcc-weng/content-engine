## 2. His Content Formula (Deconstructed)

### The Script Template (30-60 seconds)

Every video follows one of 4 templates:

**Template A: "Don't Do X" (Contrarian)**
> "You should NOT be getting a $600 Mac Mini to start running OpenClaw. Here's why."
- Hook: Contrarian statement that challenges common behavior
- Body: Problem → Why it's wrong → Better alternative
- CTA: "Comment [keyword] and I'll send you the guide"
- **Best performer:** 560K TikTok, 1.4M IG

**Template B: "X in Y Minutes" (Time ROI)**
> "Most people don't realize it only takes 30 minutes to set up a complete AI workflow that saves you 5+ hours every day."
- Hook: Time investment vs. massive return
- Body: Numbered steps (tease "step 4 is most important")
- CTA: "Save this" + "Comment [keyword]"
- **Best performer:** 312K TikTok

**Template C: "I Tested X, Here's What Matters" (Curation)**
> "There are over 60,000 Claude skills. I tested over 100. Here are the only ones that matter."
- Hook: Huge number filtered down to essentials
- Body: Category-by-category breakdown with specific names + social proof (install counts)
- CTA: "Comment [keyword] for my guide"
- **Best performer:** 159K TikTok, 197K IG

**Template D: "Free Thing You Need to Know About" (Urgency/Value)**
> "Claude is giving away a free AI certification that Deloitte is paying thousands of employees to get."
- Hook: Free + high-value + social proof (big company name)
- Body: What it is → Why it's legit → Step-by-step how to get it
- CTA: "Comment [keyword] and I'll send you the link"
- **Best performer:** 613K IG, 16K likes, 7.8K comments

### The Hook Formula

His hooks always contain ONE of these elements:
1. **Dollar amount** — "$82,000 lost", "$5K/month", "$1.49 million", "$45,000 of SEO work"
2. **Contrarian statement** — "Don't buy X", "X finally replaced Y"
3. **Fear/urgency** — "This could save your job", "Don't leave your API key exposed"
4. **Specificity** — "4,096 AI agents", "60,000 skills", "5,000 free spots"
5. **Social proof** — "Deloitte is paying thousands", "ranked #1 on GitHub"

### The CTA System

Every video has a **comment keyword CTA** that:
- Drives comments (algorithm boost — his cert reel got 7.8K comments)
- Triggers DM automation (Stan Store AutoDM)
- Delivers a free guide (lead capture)
- Each video has a UNIQUE keyword: "claw", "co-work", "test", "fish", "skill", "job", "story", "web"

### Production Style
- **Talking head** to camera, well-lit, clean background
- **No B-roll** on most videos (some screen recordings for tutorials)
- **No trending audio** — original sound or classical music (Rafael Krux)
- **Captions** — always on, part of the video
- **Duration:** 30-60 seconds for short-form
- **Hashtags:** Always `#ai #tech`, nothing else
