## What Goes Viral (Top Performers)

### Pattern 1: Contrarian Reframes (Highest Engagement)
Posts that flip a common belief on its head using a parallel structure.
- "Buy shit from your friends businesses and try and get free shit from strangers. Not the other way around." (156K likes IG, 24K likes X)
- "Rich people buy time. Poor people buy stuff. Ambitious people buy skills. Lazy people buy distraction." (26K likes X)
- "The world will reward you in proportion to your courage, not your intellect" (33K likes IG, 19K likes X)

### Pattern 2: Relationship/Personal Content (Crossover Appeal)
Content about partners, friends, and personal life breaks out of the business audience.
- "Find a woman who kills drama instead of starting it..." (84K likes IG)
- "A good partner multiplies your strengths. A bad one multiplies your weaknesses." (64K likes IG)
- Mother's passing tribute (208K likes X — his all-time top post)

### Pattern 3: Identity-Based One-Liners
Short statements that make the reader self-identify.
- "Losers become losers by being afraid of losing." (13K likes X)
- "You don't have to be the smartest to win. You just have to be the last man standing." (15K likes IG)
- "Customers become friends faster than friends become customers." (38K likes IG, 16K likes X)

### Pattern 4: Tactical Business Frameworks (Lower Likes, Higher Saves)
Framework-dense posts get fewer likes but more saves/bookmarks — higher intent audience.
- "3 types of business problems: 1) Not enough customers 2) Don't keep customers 3) Don't make enough per customer" (15K likes IG)
- "17 lessons you must learn if you want to be a top 1% salesperson" (5.6K likes IG)
- Close Rate vs Pricing Rules breakdown (12K likes IG)

### Pattern 5: AI/Tech Takes (Timely + Controversial)
AI-related posts perform above average because they're timely and polarizing.
- "More people will lose their jobs to laziness than to AI this year" (4K likes IG)
- "In a world of infinite AI labor, the last valuable thing a human will get paid to do will be to take risk." (19K likes IG)
- "The memo we sent to our company about AI writing…" (25K likes IG)

## Content Format Distribution (Instagram)

|------|-------|---|-----------|
| Reel (short video) | 112 | 93% | ~10K |

**Key finding:** Carousels dramatically outperform reels on likes (2.4x), though reels get more views. His top 3 non-viral posts are all carousels.

## Cross-Platform Repurposing Pattern

Many posts appear identically across platforms:
- "Buy shit from your friends businesses..." — #1 on both IG and X
- "Customers become friends faster..." — Top 5 on both
- "The world will reward you in proportion to your courage..." — Top 10 on both

**Strategy:** He writes once, posts everywhere. The same one-liner appears on IG (as text-over-image reel or carousel), X (as tweet), LinkedIn (as text post), and TikTok (as talking-head clip).

## CTA Patterns

Two dominant CTA patterns:
1. **Comment trigger:** "Comment 'MAP' and I'll send you our free scaling roadmap" — drives comments which boost algorithm
2. **Follow CTA:** "Follow for more business education" — embedded in caption, not separate

Comment-trigger CTAs appear in ~30% of posts and consistently drive 400-1500+ comments vs ~50-100 organic.

## Implications for Mike

1. **Start with one-liners** — They're the easiest to produce and get the most engagement. Write 50 contrarian reframes about your niche.
2. **Repurpose everything** — Write once, post across all platforms with minor formatting adjustments.
3. **Carousels for depth** — When you have a framework to teach, use carousel format (higher engagement than reels).
4. **Comment CTAs** — Use "Comment X to get Y" to boost algorithmic distribution.
5. **2 posts/day minimum** — Hormozi's baseline cadence across all platforms.
6. **Personal content breaks through** — Don't be afraid to share personal stories. They outperform business content by 3-5x.
7. **AI takes are timely gold** — Your RE + AI positioning is perfectly aligned with what gets engagement right now.
