# Hook Types

8 hook types for multi-platform posts. The skill auto-selects based on topic signals, or the user can override.

## 1. Bold Claim (偏激言論)

**Signal:** Topic is debatable — you have a strong opinion others will push back on.
**Structure:** State something provocative in the first line. No hedging.

Example openers (Threads):
- "大學根本是在浪費時間"
- "學程式最沒用的方式就是上課"
- "90%的人根本不需要學 AI"

**RED title example:**
"海外仲介不用AI｜你已經落後三年了" (keyword pull: 海外仲介 + AI, 18 chars, claim without hedging)

**Instagram slide 1 example:**
"Most agents are one AI tool away from doubling their capacity" (under 12 words, bold visual text, answers "is this for me?")

**X example:**
"Hot take: agents who don't use AI for overseas buyer comms will lose those clients to the ones who do. Not eventually. Now." (under 280 chars, scroll-stopping, invites pushback)

**LinkedIn example:**
"The Australian real estate industry is 3 years behind on AI adoption — and overseas buyer agents are feeling it most. Here's what I mean." (first 210 chars, contrarian opener, sets up a professional argument)

## 2. Pain Point (直擊痛點)

**Signal:** Topic solves a problem — name the reader's frustration directly.
**Structure:** First line = the specific pain. Reader should think "this is exactly me."

Example openers (Threads):
- "為什麼你學了三個月的程式 還是不會自己做東西"
- "每次打開 ChatGPT 都不知道要問什麼的人 看這邊"
- "花了兩萬塊上課 結果還是不會用 AI"

**RED title example:**
"海外買家仲介｜每次解釋貸款條件都解釋到崩潰嗎" (keyword: 海外買家仲介, pain: explaining mortgage conditions repeatedly, 18 chars)

**Instagram slide 1 example:**
"Explaining Australian mortgage rules to overseas buyers. Every. Single. Time." (under 12 words effectively, bold, pain recognition)

**X example:**
"Agents working with overseas buyers spend ~40% of their time re-explaining the same legal/finance info. There's an AI fix for this." (under 280 chars, names pain + hints at solution)

**LinkedIn example:**
"If you work with overseas Chinese buyers, you've had this experience: explaining the same FIRB rules, the same stamp duty breakdown, the same settlement timeline — call after call. There's a better way." (first 210 chars, specific pain, narrative opener)

## 3. Curiosity Gap (好奇心)

**Signal:** Topic has a surprise or discovery — hint at it without revealing.
**Structure:** Imply valuable info exists. Leave the gap open. Brain can't resist.

Example openers (Threads):
- "昨天發現一個功能 差點沒從椅子上摔下來"
- "有一件事我一直不敢講 但今天決定說了"
- "我終於搞懂為什麼我的貼文都沒人看了"

**RED title example:**
"發現一個AI工具｜海外仲介用了都說不會再手動做" (keyword: AI工具 + 海外仲介, curiosity gap: what is this tool?, 18 chars)

**Instagram slide 1 example:**
"I found a tool that cut my overseas buyer onboarding by 3 hours" (under 12 words, curiosity gap, specific number anchors it)

**X example:**
"Found an AI workflow that eliminates the most painful part of working with overseas buyers. Going to share exactly how I built it this week." (under 280 chars, open loop, build-in-public setup)

**LinkedIn example:**
"I stumbled on a workflow last month that changed how I help agents serving overseas buyers. I wasn't expecting it to work this well — but the numbers don't lie." (first 210 chars, curiosity gap, narrative opener that pulls to "See more")

## 4. Numbers First (數字開頭)

**Signal:** Topic has concrete metrics — results, timeframes, costs.
**Structure:** Stack numbers vertically or lead with a specific figure. Breaks visual pattern of text feeds.

Example openers (Threads):
- "2天\n0行 Swift 經驗\n1個完整的 iOS app"
- "47塊美金的 API 費用\n換來一個月省40小時的自動化"
- "90天 176則貼文 849萬次曝光"

**RED title example:**
"3小時→20分鐘｜AI幫海外買家仲介省了多少時間" (keyword: AI + 海外買家仲介, numbers up front in title, 18 chars)

**Instagram slide 1 example:**
"3 hours → 18 minutes. This is what AI did to my overseas buyer intake." (under 12 words, bold contrast, specific numbers)

**X example:**
"3 hours → 18 minutes
That's how long overseas buyer onboarding took before and after I built this AI workflow.
Here's exactly what changed:" (under 280 chars, stacked numbers, build-in-public thread opener)

**LinkedIn example:**
"3 hours. That's how long it took to onboard a new overseas buyer client before I built an AI-assisted workflow. Now it takes 18 minutes. Here's what I changed — and what I'd do differently." (first 210 chars, statistics opener, professional framing)

## 5. Reverse Contrast (逆向反差)

**Signal:** Topic has an unexpected outcome — before/after or contradiction.
**Structure:** Set up expectation, then flip it.

Example openers (Threads):
- "我以為 AI 會讓我更快\n結果第一個月反而更慢了"
- "客戶說不要用 AI\n結果他自己偷偷在用"
- "本來只是想試玩一下\n三天後辭職了"

**RED title example:**
"以為AI幫不了房產仲介｜結果我錯了" (keyword: AI + 房產仲介, sets up reversal, 16 chars including promise of payoff)

**Instagram slide 1 example:**
"I thought AI couldn't help with overseas buyer relationships. I was wrong." (under 12 words effectively, classic reversal, bold)

**X example:**
"I thought overseas buyers wouldn't trust an AI-assisted agent.
Plot twist: they preferred it.
They felt more informed, less pressured, more confident." (under 280 chars, contrast structure, specific outcome)

**LinkedIn example:**
"I assumed overseas Chinese buyers would be skeptical of AI-assisted agents. I was wrong — and the reason why changed how I think about this market entirely." (first 210 chars, reverse contrast, professional curiosity opener)

## 6. Age + Result (報年齡)

**Signal:** Topic is autobiographical with a milestone or achievement.
**Structure:** Age contextualizes everything. Creates relatability or aspiration.

Example openers (Threads):
- "我32歲才開始學寫程式"
- "28歲 月收入從3萬變30萬\n不是靠加班"
- "35歲 第一次被自己做的東西嚇到"

**RED title example:**
"我35歲買了第4間澳洲房產｜靠AI做的投資分析" (keyword: 澳洲房產 + AI, age anchors credibility, milestone up front)

**Instagram slide 1 example:**
"I bought my 4th Australian property at 35 — AI research made the difference" (under 12 words, milestone + result, personal authority)

**X example:**
"At 35 I bought my 4th Australian property. Every one was different. The AI tools I used on the 4th changed what I look for now. Thread:" (under 280 chars, age + milestone, thread opener)

**LinkedIn example:**
"I bought my fourth Australian investment property at 35. Each one taught me something new about the overseas buyer experience. The last one taught me how much AI had changed the research process." (first 210 chars, narrative opener, personal authority establishes credibility)

## 7. Mid-Action Story (故事開頭)

**Signal:** Topic is narrative-driven — something happened that's worth telling.
**Structure:** Drop the reader into a moment. No background, no setup. Start mid-action.

Example openers (Threads):
- "凌晨兩點 我盯著螢幕 手在抖"
- "客戶打來的時候我正在吃便當"
- "當我按下 deploy 的那一刻 心臟差點停了"

**RED title example:**
"深夜接到海外買家電話｜那通電話改變了我的工作方式" (keyword: 海外買家, mid-action in title, emotional hook)

**Instagram slide 1 example:**
"My sister got a call at 11pm from a buyer in Taiwan who wanted to make an offer. By midnight, AI had drafted the entire response." (bold, mid-action drop, answers "is this real?")

**X example:**
"My sister (Sydney agent) got a call from a Taiwan buyer at 11pm wanting to make an offer. She had the full translated, culturally adapted response drafted in 8 minutes. Here's the workflow:" (under 280 chars, mid-action, thread setup)

**LinkedIn example:**
"It was 11pm in Sydney. My sister — a real estate agent specializing in overseas Chinese buyers — got a call from a client in Taipei wanting to make an offer on a property that closed viewings at 9am the next morning. What happened next is why I started building AI tools for agents." (first 210 chars, mid-action narrative, stakes immediately clear)

## 8. Raw Rant (情緒性)

**Signal:** Topic triggers genuine emotion — frustration, surprise, injustice.
**Structure:** Lead with the emotion. Raw and unfiltered. Don't explain first, feel first.

Example openers (Threads):
- "靠 我真的快氣死"
- "我受夠了每次都聽到「AI 會取代你」"
- "三小啦 這個 bug 搞了我整整兩天"

**RED title example:**
"海外買家仲介崩潰日記｜這個流程真的有夠破" (keyword: 海外買家仲介, raw emotion in title, KOC authenticity)

**Instagram slide 1 example:**
"Nobody talks about how broken the overseas buyer process actually is." (under 12 words, frustration-first, bold)

**X example:**
"The overseas buyer agent workflow in 2026 is genuinely broken. Phone tag across time zones. Documents in three languages. Manual everything. Someone fix this." (under 280 chars, raw frustration, scroll-stopping)

**LinkedIn example:**
"I'll say what most people in the industry aren't saying: the current process for serving overseas property buyers is broken in ways that AI could fix tomorrow — if anyone bothered to build the right tools." (first 210 chars, professional rant, provocative enough to pull past "See more")
