# Threads Hook & Format Research (2026)

## Algorithm Signals (weighted)

| Signal | Weight | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| Engagement velocity | ~40% | 50 replies in 30 min >> 200 likes over 24h |
| Completion rate | ~25% | Do users finish reading? Structure and open loops help |
| Conversation quality | ~20% | Back-and-forth discussion >> generic reactions |
| Network relevance | ~10% | Consistent topic posting helps algo target |
| Freshness & consistency | ~5% | Regular posting > bursts then silence |

**Key difference from X:** Replies weighted far more heavily than likes. Mosseri: "The sum of all your replies is about as valuable as the sum of all your posts."

**Suppressed:** Engagement bait, external links in main post, duplicate/recycled content, overt selling.

**Taiwan = #1 market globally** (22% traffic share, 1,294% YoY growth). Posts surpassed Facebook volume in Taiwan.

## Hook Patterns

### 1. Specific Number
- "I've posted 500+ times on Threads. Here are 7 types that always go viral."

### 2. Pattern Interrupt / Contrarian
- "Stop posting carousels on Threads. Here's why they're killing your reach."

### 3. Curiosity Gap
- "This life hack saved me 10 hours a week. I'll explain in 15 seconds."

### 4. Story Opener with Specific Detail
- "Last Tuesday at 2am, a DM from a stranger changed how I think about pricing."

### 5. Direct Pain Point
- "Your Threads posts get zero engagement because you skip the first 3 seconds."

### Taiwan/Traditional Chinese Hooks
- "這間餐廳老闆瘋了..." (shock/curiosity)
- "千萬別讓你的朋友知道這招..." (exclusivity)
- Contrast: "月薪3萬 vs. 月薪30萬的早餐吃什麼？"
- Community phrasing: "不懂就問脆" / "萬能的脆演算法"
- 台語 puns and cultural wordplay

## Format Rules

- Under 500 chars for best completion (500 is the ceiling)
- Text-only works fine — this is not Instagram
- Photos get ~60% more engagement than text-only
- Short video (15-30s) + text caption = highest views
- Bullet points > paragraphs
- Emoji every ~3 lines for visual rhythm
- One topic tag per post (no # needed, can include spaces)
- End polarizing: "認同的按愛心，反對的留言罵我"

---

## What Goes Viral

1. Hot takes with reasoning (not just "X is dead" but "X is dead, and here's the data")
2. Process breakdowns with specific numbers
3. Personal stories (before/path/after)
4. Wins posts (people celebrate others' achievements)
5. Genuine conversation starters
6. Data-driven original insights with screenshots

---

## Taiwan-Specific Patterns

- Humanized voice >> corporate tone. Self-deprecation, 台語, slang perform.
- Authenticity > aesthetics (opposite of IG)
- Micro-influencers punch above weight (300-follower creator → 570K views)
- "脆" (cuì) culture — platform-native language signals insider status
- Fun first, content second: "有趣為主，內容為輔"
- Conversation hijacking — replying to trending discussions > initiating them

---

## Tactical

- Post 2-3x daily, 7-9 AM weekday mornings
- Reply to 5-10 conversations daily with substance
- 30-min engagement sprint after every post
- No links in main post — first reply
- Alternate formats: text / photo / video / carousel rotation
- Consistency > perfection
