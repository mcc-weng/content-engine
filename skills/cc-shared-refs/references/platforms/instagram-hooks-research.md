# Instagram Hook & Format Research (2026)

## Format Performance

| Format | Engagement Rate | Best For |
|--------|----------------|----------|
| Carousels | 6.90% median | Saves, depth, authority |
| Reels | 3.31% (but 36% more reach) | Discovery, non-followers |
| Static Images | 4.44% (declining -17% YoY) | Culture posts |

**Key mechanic:** Unswiped carousel slides get re-served as "new content" — multiple chances to earn engagement. No other format does this.

**Recommended mix:** 60-70% Reels (discovery), 20-30% carousels (saves/authority), 10% static.

---

## Algorithm Signals (ranked)

1. **Sends (DM shares)** — single strongest signal
2. **Saves** — "worth coming back to"
3. **Comments** — elevated in 2026
4. **Active dwell time** — carousels maximize this
5. **Swipe-through velocity** — each swipe = engagement event
6. **Completion rate** — reaching last slide
7. Likes — weakest signal

## First Slide Hook Patterns

First slide carries 80% of the weight. Users scroll at 3-4 posts/sec. Must work in <0.7 seconds.

**Must answer:** "Is this for me?" + "What will I get if I swipe?"

**Rules:** Under 8-10 words. Readable without zooming. High contrast.

### A. Problem/Pain
- "Stop doing X"
- "The #1 mistake killing your [goal]"
- "3 signs [problem]"
- "Why your [thing] isn't working"

### B. Curiosity/Information Gap
- "I improved my _____ in 30 days. Here's how."
- "What I wish I knew before [X]"
- "The hidden cost of [common practice]"
- "Myth vs. Fact: [topic]"

### C. Greed/Value
- "Steal my [workflow/framework]"
- "The simple framework behind [impressive result]"
- "[Number] [things] that [specific outcome]"
- "How I [result] without [expected requirement]"

### D. Social Proof/Authority
- "[Specific result] in [timeframe]"
- "Before/after: [transformation]"
- "[Number] lessons from [experience]"

### E. Direct Address
- "This is for [audience] who [situation]"
- "If you're a [role] struggling with [problem], read this"

### F. Contrarian
- "[Accepted thing] is wrong. Here's why."
- "I stopped [common practice] and [unexpected result]"

**Design tricks:** Cropped diagrams, partial checklists, split-screen — make slide feel visually incomplete to drive swipe. "Swipe left" prompt increases engagement.

## Carousel Structure

**Optimal count:** 8-12 slides (educational), 3-5 (quick tips)

| Slide | Purpose |
|-------|---------|
| 1 | **Hook** — bold statement/question, under 10 words |
| 2 | **Context/Credibility** — "why this matters" or proof |
| 3-8 | **Value** — one idea per slide, bold key phrases |
| 9 | **Summary/Recap** — reinforce key takeaway |
| 10 | **CTA** — save, share, or question for comments |

**Per-slide rules:**
- One headline + one subhead max
- No paragraphs — design for skimmers
- Large enough to read on mobile
- Consistent branding (fonts, colors)
- 1080x1350px (4:5) or 1080x1440px (3:4)

---

## Caption Strategy

- First line = second hook (shows before "more" truncation)
- Write like mini-blog post — expand carousel, don't repeat it
- Primary keywords in opening sentence (Instagram indexes captions for search)
- Specific CTA: ask for saves, shares, or pose question
- Keywords now drive more reach than hashtags alone

---

## Hashtag Strategy

3-5 hashtags using the "1-2-2 Rule":
- 1 broad industry tag (#RealEstateTech)
- 2 specific niche tags (#PropTechAI, #AIforAgents)
- 2 contextual/community tags (#BuildInPublic, #MelbourneRealEstate)

Target tags with 50K-500K posts. Avoid 10M+ (content buried).

---

## Niche Carousel Ideas (AI / PropTech)

- "What I wish I knew at the start" — each slide = a lesson
- "Steal my workflow" — actual process with time blocks
- "Before/after transformations" — manual vs AI workflow
- "Myth vs Fact: AI in real estate"
- "Hidden costs of [manual process]" — quantify wasted time
- Tutorial: "Set up automated lead follow-up in 10 minutes"
- Data-driven: "We analyzed 500 agent response times. Here's what we found."

---

## Tactical

- 3-5 carousels/week as core content
- Carousels underutilized relative to performance — high-leverage now
- Priority: First slide hook > saves/sends > CTA > caption > hashtags
