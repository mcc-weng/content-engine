# LinkedIn Hook & Format Research (2026)

## Algorithm Signals (ranked)

1. Comments (15+ words) — weighted 15x more than likes
2. Saves and Sends — new high-value signals
3. Dwell time (30 sec reading > 50 quick likes)
4. Topic relevance — LinkedIn checks if post matches your profile expertise
5. Likes — least valuable

**Golden hour:** First 60-90 min = 70% of total reach. Reply to comments within 15 min.

**Reach killers:** Links in body (-60%), 5+ hashtags, editing after publish, multiple posts <24h apart, engagement bait phrases, dense text walls.

**Format performance:** Carousels/PDFs 24.42% engagement (4x text-only) > Multi-image 6.6% > Video 5.6% > Polls 4.4-12% > Text-only ~4% > Single images (down 30%).

**Optimal length:** 900-1,500 chars. First 210 chars = "See more" cutoff.

## Hook Categories (with templates)

### 1. Contrarian / Bold Statement (highest comment-driver)
- "Unpopular opinion: [contrarian statement]"
- "[Conventional wisdom] is wrong. Here's what actually works:"
- "Stop [common behavior]. Here's why:"
- "Hard truth: [uncomfortable reality]."
- "'More content' is terrible LinkedIn advice. Here's what actually grows your audience:"

### 2. Specific Number / Data
- "I analyzed [X] [things]. Here's what [audience] needs to know:"
- "Only [X]% of [group] do [thing]. Here's why:"
- "We spent $[amount] testing [strategies]. Here's what worked:"
- "I tracked [metric] for [timeframe]. The results surprised me:"

### 3. Confession / Vulnerability (failure stories get 6x more comments than success)
- "For [timeframe], I [struggle]. Then I discovered [solution]:"
- "I almost gave up after [setback]. Here's what changed:"
- "My biggest failure turned into my most treasured success."

### 4. Open Loop / Mystery
- "This one sentence 3x'd our reply rate."
- "We thought it was a bad idea. We were wrong."
- "I showed a real estate agent our AI tool. Her reaction surprised me."

### 5. Results / Before-After
- "[Time ago], [past situation]. Today, [current situation]."
- "Cut churn by 30% — without changing the product."
- "Before: 14 missed leads per month. After: zero. One automation."

### 6. Question
- "Are you still measuring [metric] instead of [better metric]?"
- "What if I told you [surprising claim]?"

### 7. Prediction
- "By [year], [bold prediction]. Here's why I'm confident:"
- "Popular take: [X]. My prediction? [Y]."

## Post Structures

### Story Post (highest comment potential)
```
[Opening scene — specific moment]
[Context — what led here]
[Turning point — what changed]
[Lesson — universal insight]
[Takeaway for reader]
```

### Before/After
```
[Time ago], [past situation with numbers].
Today, [current situation with numbers].
The shift: [Key changes]
Same person. Different approach.
[Question]
```

### Contrarian Take
```
Unpopular opinion: [Statement]
Most [audience] believe [conventional wisdom].
But here's what the data shows:
-> [Evidence 1]
-> [Evidence 2]
-> [Evidence 3]
The real question isn't [common]. It's [reframed].
Agree or disagree?
```

### Behind-the-Scenes / Build-in-Public
```
What [building X] actually looks like:
What social media shows: [polished]
The reality: [honest description]
I'm sharing this because [reason].
[Question about reader's experience]
```

### Myth Buster
```
[Number] [topic] myths I wish I'd stopped believing:
Myth 1: "[belief]" → Reality: [truth]
Myth 2: "[belief]" → Reality: [truth]
Which of these surprised you?
```

## Formatting Rules

- 8-12 words max per line
- 1-3 line paragraphs, then blank line (mobile readability)
- Use arrows (`->`) for lists, not bullet points
- No external links in body (first comment only)
- 3-5 hashtags at bottom
- End with specific question (not "what do you think?")
- Best times: Tue-Thu, 10-11 AM audience timezone
- 3-5 posts/week, spaced 24-48h apart

## Niche Insights (AI / PropTech / Build-in-Public)

- Build-in-public story posts (vulnerability + results) = sweet spot for solo founders
- PropTech contrarian takes cut through hype — gets saved and shared
- Failure stories outperform success stories by ~6x in comments
- More niche = better — "AI for real estate follow-ups" > "AI tools"
- Carousels for frameworks get 4x engagement vs text
