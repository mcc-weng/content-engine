# CTA Bank

Categorized CTA patterns by platform. Used as fallback when the post's natural ending doesn't invite conversation.

## Selection Rules

1. **Default: integrated ending.** If the post naturally invites response (hot takes, rants, question posts), don't add an explicit CTA. The content IS the CTA.
2. **Fallback: pick from bank.** When the topic doesn't naturally invite conversation (demo posts, curated insights), select the best-fit pattern below.
3. **Never repeat.** Don't use the same CTA type in consecutive posts. Check last 2-3 drafted posts in ideas.md.
4. **Anti-spam.** If the CTA feels bolted on, use integrated ending instead. Platforms penalize engagement bait.
5. **Platform determines CTA style:** saves for RED/Instagram, replies for Threads/X, comments for LinkedIn. Never use Chinese CTAs on English platforms or vice versa.

## Threads (Traditional Chinese)

### Experience-Sharing
Invite people to share their own version of the story.

- "你有沒有類似的經驗？"
- "有人也是這樣嗎"
- "你的版本是什麼"

**Best for:** Personal journey, rant, relatable struggles

### Opinion Poll
Quick A/B choice that's easy to answer.

- "A還是B？你選哪個"
- "你覺得是X還是Y"
- "猜猜看結果是什麼"

**Best for:** Comparisons, decisions, predictions

### Challenge
Dare the reader to try something.

- "不信你試試看"
- "今天就花10分鐘試一次"
- "你敢的話留言你的結果"

**Best for:** Demos, tools, actionable tips

### Prediction
Frame your take as a bet and invite disagreement.

- "我猜大部分人會選X 你呢？"
- "我賭三年內會變成這樣"
- "不同意的來跟我吵"

**Best for:** Hot takes, trend observations, bold claims

### Genuine Curiosity
Ask something you actually want to know.

- "真心想知道..."
- "有沒有人可以解釋一下"
- "我到現在還想不通 有人懂嗎"

**Best for:** Question posts, observations, things that puzzle you

### What NOT to Do (Threads)

- "你覺得呢？" as a default — too generic, overused
- "Like if you agree" — Meta is actively penalizing this
- "留言 [word] 送你懶人包" — works short-term, increasingly suppressed
- "Tag a friend" — disconnected engagement bait
- Any CTA that exists to trick people into engaging rather than genuinely inviting conversation

## RED (Traditional Chinese)

Primary signal on RED is **saves** — every CTA should make the reader feel the content is worth coming back to.

### Save Trigger
Make the reader feel this note is reference material they'll need later.

- "收藏這篇，之後看房的時候會用到"
- "先存起來，等你開始找海外仲介的時候翻出來"
- "這篇整理了很久，收藏比留言更有用"

**Best for:** Guides, tool roundups, process breakdowns

### Bookmark + Return Prompt
Pairs a save with a future use case.

- "先收藏，看完再回來告訴我你覺得哪個最難"
- "存起來，等你真的開始看澳洲房的時候再看一遍"
- "這種資訊隔一段時間會需要重看，先收"

**Best for:** Research notes, buying process explainers, market overviews

### Engagement Question (Save-Compatible)
Question that invites replies AND reinforces save value.

- "你現在在哪個買房階段？收藏這篇，在留言區說說"
- "有在用AI工具的仲介嗎？分享一下你怎麼用的"
- "這幾個問題你遇過幾個？留言告訴我，也歡迎補充"

**Best for:** Educational posts, process guides, overseas buyer tips

### Knowledge-Sharing Invite
Ask readers to share expertise — builds community.

- "有仲介朋友用過更好的工具嗎？在留言告訴我"
- "有沒有更快的方法？歡迎在下面補充"

**Best for:** Tool recommendations, workflow questions

### What NOT to Do (RED)

- Generic "你覺得呢?" without a real question — too weak for RED's algorithm
- Pure engagement bait with no save value — RED suppresses notes with high comment but low save ratio
- Hashtag spam (5-8 relevant hashtags max, not 20+)

## Instagram (English)

Primary signals on Instagram are **saves and DMs** — carousels succeed when people save for reference or DM for more.

### Save Driver
Direct save ask tied to a specific future use case.

- "Save this for when you're ready to automate your agent workflow"
- "Save this — it's the checklist I wish I had when I started"
- "Save this before you take your next overseas buyer client"

**Best for:** Process guides, checklists, tool breakdowns

### DM Keyword
Low-friction way to request a resource or deeper breakdown.

- "DM me 'AGENT' and I'll send you the full workflow doc"
- "DM me 'TOOLS' for the full list of what I use"
- "DM me 'OVERSEAS' for the breakdown I couldn't fit in slides"

**Best for:** Lead generation, resource sharing, detailed guides

### Share Prompt
Ask for a targeted share to a specific person.

- "Share this with an agent who's still doing this manually"
- "Tag an agent friend who's working with overseas buyers"
- "Send this to the most overworked agent you know"

**Best for:** Posts about industry pain points, broken workflows

### Comment Emoji / Quick Response
Ultra-low-friction engagement that works with Instagram algorithm.

- "Drop a 🏠 if you're working with overseas buyers"
- "Comment 'YES' if you've had this exact problem"
- "React with ✅ if you're already doing this"

**Best for:** Validating common experiences, community building

### What NOT to Do (Instagram)

- "Like and follow for more" — lowest performing CTA on carousels
- Long-form questions on the final slide — friction kills action
- Asking for multiple things (save AND comment AND share) — pick one

## X (English)

Primary signal on X is **replies and quote-tweets** — build-in-public content succeeds when it invites reactions and conversations.

### Reply Driver
Ask a specific question that people in your niche actually want to answer.

- "What's the most time-consuming part of your overseas buyer workflow?"
- "Which AI tool has saved you the most time as an agent? I'm building a list."
- "What's the one thing you wish clients understood before the first call?"

**Best for:** Question posts, observation posts, community building

### Build-in-Public Engagement
Frame the post as public progress that invites the community in.

- "Following me while I build this — happy to share everything I learn"
- "I'm figuring this out in public. If you're doing the same, let's compare notes."
- "Week 3 of building AI tools for agents. Here's what surprised me."

**Best for:** Process posts, iteration updates, workflow reveals

### Quote-Tweet Invite
Invites people to share their take or repost with commentary.

- "Quote this with your AI hot take for agents"
- "Quote with your version of this workflow"
- "Disagree? Quote me with your counter."

**Best for:** Hot takes, bold claims, polarizing observations

### Thread Continuation
Drives follows and profile visits from thread readers.

- "Follow me — I'm building this in public and sharing everything"
- "More of this coming. Follow so you don't miss the breakdown."
- "I'll share the full workflow in tomorrow's thread."

**Best for:** Thread starters, build-in-public series

### What NOT to Do (X)

- "Retweet if you agree" — suppressed and outdated
- Asking for replies with no specific angle — "thoughts?" invites nothing
- Thread CTAs that promise a continuation that never comes — kills trust

## LinkedIn (English)

Primary signal on LinkedIn is **comments** — posts succeed when they trigger professional expertise-sharing and discussion.

### Expertise Invite
Ask a question that lets professionals share what they know.

- "What's your biggest bottleneck when working with overseas buyers?"
- "If you've automated any part of this, I'd love to hear how."
- "Which part of this process have you cracked? Share below."

**Best for:** Process posts, tool posts, workflow breakdowns

### Experience-Sharing Question
Invite peers to compare notes on a shared professional experience.

- "How are you handling the language and culture gap with overseas clients?"
- "What changed for you when you first started using AI in your practice?"
- "Has this happened to you? I'm curious if it's an industry-wide thing."

**Best for:** Personal journey posts, industry observation posts

### Connection Prompt
Invite relevant professionals to connect directly.

- "If you're in Australian PropTech, let's connect — I'm building in this space."
- "If you work with overseas buyers and want to compare notes, reach out."
- "Building tools for agents in this space. Would love to hear from people working the problem."

**Best for:** Industry positioning posts, build-in-public posts

### Industry Discussion Starter
Frame the post as opening a professional debate.

- "I don't think there's a right answer here — curious what practitioners think."
- "This is my current thinking, but I suspect others see it differently. What am I missing?"
- "Would love a reality check from agents who are further along than I am."

**Best for:** Hot takes, observation posts, contrarian opinions

### What NOT to Do (LinkedIn)

- "Agree? Leave a comment" — too generic, no expertise trigger
- Long multi-part questions — pick one specific angle
- Asking for likes — LinkedIn's algorithm doesn't reward like-baiting
- "Feel free to reach out" with no specific reason to — too passive
